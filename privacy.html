Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

This is the personal website of Paolo Ciotta, who is responsible for it. Questions about your data: paolociotta1@gmail.com.

In short

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Fonts and images are served from this website; your browser does not contact Google or other third parties while you browse.
  • If you use the “Ask about me” assistant, your questions are sent to Anthropic to generate the answer.

Stored in your browser

The site remembers your chosen language and which sections you have already visited, using your browser’s local storage. This data never leaves your device and you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

When you visit

Like any website, the server receives your IP address to deliver the pages. The web server does not keep access logs. The assistant keeps your IP address in memory only, to limit how many questions can be sent per minute and per day (protection against abuse). It is never written to disk and is deleted at the end of each day (UTC). Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and available (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The “Ask about me” assistant

The assistant is optional. When you send a question, the question and the last few messages of the conversation are sent to Anthropic (the provider of the Claude AI models), which processes them to generate the answer. Your IP address is not sent to Anthropic. The conversation is not stored on this website and disappears when you close the page; question texts are not logged. Anthropic handles API data according to its privacy policy and commercial terms. Please don’t include personal or sensitive information in your questions. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub projects

The list of projects is fetched from GitHub by the server, not by your browser. Only if the server is unreachable does your browser ask GitHub’s public API directly, which shares your IP address with GitHub.

Links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub or e-mail are only followed if you click them; those services then apply their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to paolociotta1@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
